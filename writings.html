Sockdolager's

You don't have to live like shattered glass wishing to be together again. The powerful believe in power because they have armies that walk on earth. The people are powerful because they are the world. The struggles of life are constant, if you carry that weight, you'll never know what it's like to fly. It's ok to slow down and rest. A flame doesn't rush to burn the log. A god complex is only real if they have no prayers to be answered. Don't be fooled by your self righteousness as you become better in life, always remember it's better to be human from the ground and not the sky. We never really know if the light were chasing is the light of heaven or hell. Something that sounds stupid that stops a fight is greater then the perfect words. An action caused by words is far greater then it's quote.
